AutoClicker is installed.

Open it from Applications (or the Dock or Desktop, if you chose those). macOS then asks for two permissions. Allow AutoClicker under System Settings > Privacy & Security:

  • Accessibility, for clicking and Pick location.
  • Input Monitoring, for the F6 hotkey, Esc and recording.

If macOS says it can't verify the developer when you open it, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

To uninstall, drag AutoClicker from Applications to the Trash, then drag it off the Dock and delete the Desktop link.
